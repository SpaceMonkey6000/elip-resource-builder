Step 1 · the initial filter

Your resume is a client-ready deliverable.

Screeners spend 6–30 seconds. It must telegraph structured thinking and impact instantly — and survive the ATS. Pair this with the universal Resume Playbook.

Step 3 · the weekly drill

Drill micro-skills in isolation. Then integrate.

A case is a stack of micro-skills: math setup, market sizing, issue trees, chart reading, brainstorming, synthesis. Practicing them separately beats aimlessly reading solved cases.

1
Diagnose

Full case with a peer

Establish a baseline; identify your single most pressing weakness (structuring? mental math?).

2
Remediate

Study that one gap

Framework guides and video walkthroughs targeting exactly that weakness.

3
Drill A

Isolated rep

20 minutes drawing issue trees for market-entry scenarios — no math, just structure.

4
Drill B

Different archetype

Repeat on a new industry so the skill is adaptable, not memorized.

5
Apply

Mini-case

Use the remediated skill in a 20-minute case segment under mild time pressure.

6
Integrate

Full 45-min mock

Realistic, timed — test flow, endurance and synthesis end-to-end.

7
Review

Compare & adjust

Analyze the feedback vs. Day 1, measure improvement, set next week's agenda.

Step 4 · the digital screen

The robot screens you before a human does.

Gamified chatbot assessments are now the first scalable filter. Know the format cold.

🟢 BCG Casey

  • 6–10 mixed prompts in ~25–35 min via a chat interface.
  • Opens with dataset selection — filter noise, apply judgment.
  • No negative marking → never leave a question blank; eliminate and guess.
  • Ends with a 60s-prep / 60s-record video: answer-first, 2–3 supports, risks + next steps.

🔵 McKinsey Solve

  • Gamified ecosystem/scenario simulations (e.g. the "ecosystem building" game).
  • Tests structured reasoning & data handling under a strict timer.
  • No pausing, no going back — rewards rapid, sound analytical processing.
  • Practice the format so the mechanics don't cost you on the day.
